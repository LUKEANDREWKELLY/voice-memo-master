    Every memo gets the same chain: cut the talking and handling noise before and after the song, reduce room hiss, voice EQ for clarity, compression, then a loud master at −10 LUFS with a −1.5 dB ceiling. Move the sliders to hear changes live on the master, then hit Apply to bake them in; Volume moves the master’s loudness from −22 to −7 LUFS. Drag a slider sideways or up and down, scroll it, or use the arrow keys; double-click resets it. Memos are saved in this browser on this device. A single master downloads as a WAV (on iPhone it opens the share sheet, so you can Save to Files); Download all masters gives you a .zip of WAVs. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app; it works offline once loaded. On a computer you can also drag memos onto the drop box.
