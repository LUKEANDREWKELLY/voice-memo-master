Every song gets the same treatment: it tidies up the start and end (bye-bye chatter and fumbling before and after you play), cleans up hiss, gives your voice a little shine, and makes every song sit at the same loudness. Slide LOUD, WARMTH and SPARKLE to hear the change right away, then tap KEEP IT to save that sound. TAPE makes it sound like an old cassette. PHONE makes it clear and punchy on tiny phone speakers. Drag a slider sideways or up and down, scroll it, or use the arrow keys; double-click puts it back to 0. Tap RAW or POLISHED to compare. Your songs get dealt at random into up to 6 buckets: tap a tab to open one, SHUFFLE deals again, NEXT and SURPRISE ME hop around, PLAY BUCKET plays them one after another, and ALL shows everything. RANDOMIZE (the dice) tries a random sound on every song, or tap a song’s own dice. It’s only a preview until you tap KEEP IT, and RESET puts the saved sound back. Turn off SURPRISE ON OPEN if you don’t want fresh dice each time the app opens. Sort with A–Z, NEWEST, OLDEST (by recording date) or TOP (most upvotes); PLAY BUCKET plays in that order. The black bar at the top always shows what’s playing: tap the name to jump to that song, tap the thin line to skip around, NEXT for the next song. Songs are named from the title saved inside the file; Voice Memos code names like “20240115 143022” become the recording date. In EDIT LIST you can tick songs and DELETE SELECTED, or tap FIX NAMES. ADD PHOTOS at the top keeps a row of your pictures; tap one to see it big, and in EDIT LIST you can move or delete them. Your songs stay saved in this browser on this device. Download one song (on iPhone the share sheet pops up so you can Save to Files), or grab them all at once in a .zip. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app; it works offline once it has loaded. On a computer you can also drag memos onto the drop box.

    Sound quality

    Each shelf is a free GitHub Pages site (about 1 GB each). Songs fill shelf 1 to about 900 MB, then move on to shelf 2, then 3. Listeners never notice.

    Your GitHub key

    1. Open GitHub’s new key page (sign in if it asks).
    2. Name it “Voice Memo Master publish” and pick when it expires.
    3. Under Repository access choose Only select repositories and pick all three: voice-memo-master, voice-memo-gallery-2 and voice-memo-gallery-3.
    4. Under Permissions → Repository permissions, set Contents to Read and write. Leave everything else alone.
    5. Tap Generate token, copy it, paste it here and tap Save key.

    The key stays only in this browser on this device. It’s never put on the site and only ever sent to api.github.com.

    More

    To publish just some songs, tap the Pub arrow on a song, or tick songs (Select / delete) and tap Publish selected. Tap Pub again on a published song to take it off. Publishing again skips songs that are already up, so if it stops halfway, just tap Publish again.
